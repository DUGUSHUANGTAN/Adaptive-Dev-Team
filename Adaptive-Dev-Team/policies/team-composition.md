# Team Composition Policy

## Minimum Sufficient Team Principle

Teams are formed using the "Minimum Sufficient Team" principle:
- Recruit only the exact number of agents needed to complete the work
- Avoid unnecessary complexity from overstaffing
- Continuously evaluate whether existing agents could handle additional work
- Remove agents when their purpose has been fulfilled

## Agent Types and Dynamic Composition

### Agent Types
- **Specialists**: Domain experts with deep knowledge
- **Builders**: Implementers who translate plans into working solutions
- **Architects**: Design system structures and integrations
- **Reviewers**: Quality assurance and code reviewers
- **Planners**: Coordinate workflows and manage dependencies
- **Leaders**: Make strategic decisions and resolve conflicts

### Dynamic Decision Criteria
Agent counts are determined by:
- Complexity and scope of the task
- Required expertise areas
- Timeline constraints
- Resource availability
- Quality requirements

## Preventing Agent Explosion

### Automatic Decommissioning
- Agents become inactive when tasks complete
- Regular reviews of agent necessity
- Consolidation of overlapping roles
- Retirement when demand drops below threshold

### Role Management
- Clear role definitions with no ambiguity
- No duplicate capabilities
- Hierarchical organization (Leader → Planner → Builders)
- Skip-level relationships only when justified

## Agent Value Justification

Each agent must demonstrate continuous value:
- Active contribution to completed work
- Unique expertise not available elsewhere
- Ability to take ownership of tasks
- Adherence to team standards and processes
- Evidence of productivity metrics

## Spawn Depth Guidelines

### Maximum Depth
- Leader → Specialist (default)
- Leader → Builder Lead → Builders (complex projects)
- No deeper than three levels

### Justification Required
For deeper hierarchies:
- High complexity of work
- Multiple interdependent systems
- Specialized coordination needs
- Risk mitigation requirements

## Anti-Abuse Measures

### Duplicate Roles
- No overlapping responsibilities
- Clear handoff points
- Single owner for each capability

### Unnecessary Complexity
- Flat structure preferred over hierarchy
- Additional agents only when justified
- Regular audits of team composition

## Team Growth Rules

### Expansion Triggers
- Increased workload complexity
- Specialized expertise gaps
- Timeline pressures
- Quality requirements escalation

### Contraction Triggers
- Completed work
- Reduced scope
- Merged capabilities
- Budget constraints
